# Manuscript extension: learning sustained contacts from observable telemetry

This is an additive manuscript section for the v7 experiment, not a relabeling of the original E1–E3 results. The original manuscript's numerical claims must retain the corrections in the independent review and v3 reproducibility report. The v7 motion population and evaluation task differ from the original candidate-record E3 classification task and the controlled v5/v6 campaigns; their F1 values cannot be compared as if they used the same benchmark.

## Methods

We evaluated learned contact reconstruction using RSSI telemetry alone and six additional observation budgets: two- or four-receiver angle-of-arrival measurements, phase-based ranging (PBR), corrected round-trip time (RTT), PBR with an RTT consistency gate, and ultra-wideband double-sided two-way time-of-flight (UWB DS-TWR). These measurements were generated by explicit synthetic sensor models. The PBR and RTT mechanisms are motivated by [Bluetooth Channel Sounding](https://www.bluetooth.com/learn-about-bluetooth/feature-enhancements/channel-sounding/), and angular observations by [Bluetooth Direction Finding](https://www.bluetooth.com/learn-about-bluetooth/feature-enhancements/direction-finding/). The implementation is an abstract mechanism comparison rather than a standards-compliant radio stack or hardware measurement.

Each six-minute recording contained eight moving tags, four gateways and a public two-room floor plan. Ward encounters, doorway rounds and near-threshold encounters randomized positions, durations and tag assignments. LOS and wall-multipath conditions shared the same motion realization. All 28 unordered pairs were represented at every one-second sample, including pairs not detected by a conventional contact rule. The instantaneous training target was true separation at most 2 m in the same room. The primary evaluation target required this condition for at least 30 consecutive elapsed seconds, or 31 samples. Short physical encounters remained positive frame labels but did not qualify as sustained reference contacts.

Models received 29 numerical observable features derived from causal RSSI, angular or ranging histories and estimated positions. Features excluded true positions, rooms, distances, identities, seeds and future observations. The public floor plan supplied an estimated same-room feature. An unavailable pair-position estimate forced nonpositive output. Standardized logistic regression, random forest, histogram gradient boosting and Gaussian naive Bayes were trained separately for each modality. Gaussian class means, variances and priors were learned; this model is distinct from the fixed Bayesian likelihood tables in the original experiment. A causal Markov extension learned transition frequencies from training labels. Its likelihood approximation does not guarantee independent emissions or calibrated temporal probabilities.

The 24 training, eight validation and eight probability-calibration motion seeds were disjoint. Classifier fitting used training frames sampled every five seconds; calibration and validation retained all seconds. Preprocessing was fitted on training data. Separate calibration recordings fitted a sigmoid probability map. Validation selected hyperparameters, a probability threshold and strict or causal seven-of-ten occupancy decoding using reconstructed-contact F1. An equally tuned distance-rule comparator selected estimated-distance thresholds of 1.5, 2, 2.5 or 3 m and the same decoder options. These thresholds did not alter the fixed 2 m reference target. Two refitted ablations removed temporal contact-history features or the estimated-room feature.

All model artifacts and decision settings were frozen before generating 30 primary held-out recordings and 12 independent-waypoint recordings. The latter assessed unseen motion on the same map, rather than a new hospital. The primary test contained 176 unique sustained contacts, and the unseen-motion test contained 68; each was observed under two matched sensor conditions. Contact matching required the same pair, at least 5 s temporal overlap and interval intersection-over-union at least 0.2, with greedy one-to-one matching. F1 was averaged across conditions within seed, then across supported seeds. Empty-reference false alarms were included. Frame Brier/ECE retained every sampled pair. Paired bootstrap intervals used 2,000 seed-level resamples and are exploratory pointwise intervals without multiplicity correction.

Every primary classifier in this table was selected using validation rather than test ranking. For example, boosted trees slightly exceeded the selected forest on four-angle test F1, and the RTT forest exceeded the selected logistic model; neither difference was used to change the prespecified selection. All families, controls and ablations remain reported in the full artifact tables.

RSSI learning recovered contacts that were absent from the tuned geometric detector, but produced 464 false sessions alongside 217 true positives and 135 false negatives when pooling the two conditions. These counts repeat each underlying motion realization across conditions. Its missed qualified-contact time remained 44.2%, and unseen-motion F1 dropped to 0.290. Thus a numerical F1 improvement did not establish a sufficiently reliable low-cost hospital system.

The four-angle selected forest produced 285 true positives, 196 false positives and 67 false negatives across conditions, with mean seed F1 0.704. Under the assumed UWB errors and ranging schedule, selected logistic regression attained F1 0.763 and 261/90/91 TP/FP/FN. UWB's incremental learned-versus-rule interval included zero; it therefore did not demonstrate a clear additional ML benefit over its tuned geometric rule under this protocol. The fixed 2 m temporal UWB rule itself attained F1 0.742.

Wall multipath reduced selected-model performance in every modality: for example, PBR decreased from LOS F1 0.768 to wall-condition F1 0.611, and UWB from 0.834 to 0.691. PBR with the RTT consistency gate did not outperform PBR alone on the primary test. The learned Markov extension reduced primary test F1 relative to each modality's selected classifier. Removing contact history reduced RSSI F1 from 0.445 to 0.279, but the UWB ablation reached 0.769 versus the selected full model's 0.763. Feature-history benefits were therefore modality dependent.

Selected-model mean frame Brier scores were 0.064, 0.049, 0.033, 0.029, 0.046, 0.029 and 0.024 across the table's modalities, compared with 0.077 for the training-prior baseline. Frame ECE ranged from 0.012 to 0.038. These probabilities concern instantaneous synthetic proximity, not sustained-session correctness or infection. Correlated-frame reliability plots are descriptive, not independent-sample confidence evidence.

## Discussion and limitations

The contribution is a reproducible comparison of observation budgets and learned contact reconstruction under matched synthetic conditions, with explicit separation between sensing, inference and hidden labels. Standard classifier families are established methods; algorithmic novelty and real-world superiority should not be claimed from this benchmark alone. The improvement arises partly from learning observable evidence that a strict noisy-distance threshold discards. Models still miss substantial contact time and generate false alarms, especially for inexpensive RSSI-only observations.

Receiver upgrades and active ranging require different hardware, scheduling and energy budgets. Four angular receivers do not imply four antennas on every patient; AoA requires appropriate direction-finding tags and receiver arrays. BLE Channel Sounding and UWB require compatible radios. The v6 capacity experiment reported degradation at assumed higher service loads, whereas the v7 ML experiment trains only on the eight-client setting. Host CPU timings do not establish embedded energy, wearable battery life or complete system latency. No procurement-optimal solution follows from these results.

Synthetic channel assumptions, the fixed two-room map and the small number of moving tags limit transportability. The unseen-motion challenge retains the same channel assumptions and floor plan. Hardware-calibrated errors, denser trajectories, different maps and independent hospital data remain necessary for deployment claims. Same-room 2 m proximity is an operational simulation definition, not a medically validated exposure criterion. The frozen artifacts and negative findings support reproducibility and scrutiny but do not alone establish scientific or clinical validity.
